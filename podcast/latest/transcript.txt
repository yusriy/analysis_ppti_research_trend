PPTI Research Brief — October 2026

Welcome to the PPTI Research Brief for October 2026. This edition covers the rolling twenty-four-month period ending 31 October 2026. The portfolio contains 570 distinct Scopus-indexed publications from 64 PPTI contributors, appearing across 315 journals. Open-access articles account for 43.5 percent of the portfolio. In the latest completed month, publication output held steady from 11 to 11 articles. Compared with the preceding monthly edition, the rolling portfolio recorded an increase of 11 publications and an increase of 104 citations. Three journals represented in the latest completed month are ACS Omega, Clean Technologies and Environmental Policy, and Journal of Environmental Chemical Engineering. Three recurring themes represented that month are Chitosan, Shelf Life, and Digestibility. They appear in 9, 8, and 6 rolling-window publications, respectively.
